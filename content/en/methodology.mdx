# Methodology

Goal: publish guides that work in real workflows (retries, error handling, limits, and total cost).

## Typical checklist

- features (routers, iterators, webhooks, scheduling)
- limits & quotas (operations, payloads, rate limits)
- reliability (logs, monitoring, retries)
- pricing (tiers, options, real usage)
